# Sauron helmet — printable paper-fit templates

**Provisional nominal 540 mm headband, not measured child fit. Start with paper, not EVA.** Original simplified drawings based on production-helmet photographs: layered face plates and six principal crown spires, without intricate engraving or a closed shell.

## Line key

- **Solid black:** cut outline and openings.
- **Dashed blue:** placement or bond/overlap guides; do not cut.
- **Dash-dot green:** shallow forming fold for paper; do not cut through foam later.
- **Gray labeled diamonds:** match the same label across parts. These are adjustable lap placements, not precision edge-to-edge joints.

## First paper assembly

1. Make the support before the face. Four H strips (165 mm each) joined at three 20 mm overlaps give **600 mm**. Close with an adjustable **60 mm rear overlap** for a nominal **540 mm** loop. Two more H strips joined with 20 mm overlap give a **310 mm** top strap; provisional 20 mm end attachments leave **270 mm** over the head. Adjust on the child, keep it loose, and keep the rear overlap reachable. No chin strap.
2. Gently curve A around the face. Match A/B to place B on its center and A/C to place both C cheeks. Wrap cheeks/temples around the face; do not just add their full flat width to the front. Cut A's eyes and B's breathing slot. Tape only B's upper A/B area, leaving its lower portion free; use the short green guide below eye level to angle it gently forward, away from nose/mouth pressure.
3. Fold K at its green center line: center its face half behind A's forehead zone and tape its band half to the front band, adjusting the fold/curvature for forehead stand-off. Turn each L 90 degrees so its 25 × 20 mm half matches A's 20 × 25 mm blue side box; tape that half directly behind A and fold the other half toward the temple band. Fit L before D, then add D behind the sides using A/D without covering L's fold or band half. Use broad outside tape areas; do not hang the face from temple tabs alone. The top strap supports height but does not prove resistance to tipping. These folds do not guarantee nose clearance; fit the wearer and recheck tipping whenever stand-off changes.
4. Add E using A/E, G using B/G, and C2 using **both** C2.1 and C2.2 diamonds. Decorative overlays are optional; do not cover sight or breathing openings.
5. Form the spires into shallow open-backed V sections along the green guides, roughly 120° as a paper starting point. Curve/fold each 25 mm root to the band and tape it in the crown position shown on page 5. Add them one at a time and check balance. S2 must not tape across the rear adjustment/removal opening. Tape or round all sharp paper corners before fitting.
6. With an adult supervising, start stationary and then walk on a clear flat floor. Check floor/side vision, normal speech/breathing, nose/ear/forehead pressure, head turns/downward looking, glasses clearance if needed, shoulder clearance, crown clearance, and **unaided removal**. Stop for slipping, obstructed sight, discomfort, dizziness, or breathing difficulty. No running or stairs in this trial.

Mark corrections on the paper: eye centers, band length, cheek length, crown height, and attachment positions. A paper fit does not establish foam fit, cured bond retention, weight, point softness, or heat comfort. Recheck these independently in a future foam prototype. This is not protective headgear or a safety certification.

## Scaling later

At the supplied size, the ruler measures 100 mm. For an initial uniform paper rescale to a measured circumference H, start with **H / 540 × 100%**; the ruler then measures **100 × H / 540 mm**. This is only a starting point: eye spacing and cheek/shoulder clearance need separate fitting, not circumference scaling.

For enlargement use a PDF viewer's **Poster / Tile Scale** printing, or resize SVG canvas and artwork together in a vector editor. Ordinary custom scaling on a fixed page can clip pieces near the margins. Keep every cutting sheet at the same intended scale, verify the appropriately scaled rulers, and check for complete outlines. Do not use Fit/Shrink as a substitute for a known scale.

## Checks and limits

All 14 distinct component outlines were checked against their declared flat dimensions, including all six spires through their quantities. The actual final PDF cutting paths, blue placement paths, green forming guides, page sizes, and red rulers were compared with the SVG masters within 0.1 mm. A deliberate copied 90%-scale cutting-path corruption was rejected with its 100 mm ruler unchanged. Drawings were rendered and visually inspected. No physical assembly, wearing test, or film-exact reconstruction has been verified.
