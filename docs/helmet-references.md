# Sauron helmet — references for original printable EVA templates

Research: 2026-10-03. Requested direction: a middle ground between the Woodland Elf craft version and highly detailed replicas, with a more movie-like silhouette and explicit printable patterns for all crown spikes. Wearer remains the same nine-year-old. This is reference research, not fitted geometry or a fabrication approval.

Evidence: the listing was fetched and the five linked gallery photographs visually inspected. The viewer configuration was fetched, but its complete rotation was not inspected. The publisher identifies the item as a Weta-made original used as reference for licensed replicas. It describes urethane construction, rubber details, leather lining, and black/silver decoration with a blue sheen. Those materials are not our child costume recipe. Published bounding dimensions are not usable child fitting dimensions or flat foam developments.

Observed features worth retaining:

- Six prominent crown spires: a tall swept side pair, a shorter forward pair, one central front spire, and one central rear spire. This replaces the earlier arbitrary five-point proposal if the revised design is approved. Small notches/points along plate edges are separate decorative features.
- Narrow central face ridge, long split lower face, angular brow, and layered swept cheek/temple plates; not just a wide flat mask with a crown attached.
- Spires have faceted/tapered depth rather than looking like rectangular strips.
- Edge ridges and overlapping panels do much of the visual work; fine etched ornament is secondary.

These are visual interpretations from perspective images, not orthographic tracings or exact measured geometry. The assembled costume should be proportioned to the child, with larger practical sight openings and softer, shorter points as needed.

## Secondary reference: licensed replica

- [United Cutlery half-scale helm, UC3521](https://www.unitedcutlery.com/ProductDetail.aspx?cat=LR&itemno=UC3521)

Manufacturer text was fetched. It describes a 15½-inch-tall polyresin display reproduction based on an original prop casting. Use only as supplementary visual context; do not enlarge a half-scale display object's dimensions to determine a child's head fit.

## Construction/detail reference, not our material recipe

- [Blind Squirrel Props: Helm of Sauron](https://www.blindsquirrelprops.com/helm-of-sauron/)

The walkthrough text was fetched and its final side-detail photograph inspected. Its layered sculpting and ridge placement are useful visual aids. Its resin/styrene spikes, dowels, epoxy work, and eye-covering insert are **not** proposed for this child build. The author's replica-size and wearing-comfort observations concern their own build, not validated measurements for ours.

## Intended next deliverable

Original EVA cutting drawings, including all six major spike positions, plus placement/assembly diagrams. A useful compromise is layered face panels and simple shallow folded/faceted foam spires, without reproducing the intricate engraving or a closed heavy shell. Exact flat developments, joint allowances, support layout, and dimensions still need a design revision and paper fitting; a projected assembly view is not a cutting template.

For practical home printing: US Letter and A4 tiled PDFs at Actual Size, full-size editable SVGs, 100 mm scale bars, page overlap/alignment marks, and component IDs, quantities, thicknesses, mirroring and fold/bond marks. Full-size must mean explicitly dimensioned geometry, not a claim of fitted sizing without measurements. If a provisional size is authorized, label every sheet as a paper-fit prototype.

Link to reference photographs and existing third-party patterns rather than redistributing them. Draw original cutting geometry. No rights to republish the source images or source pattern have been established.
